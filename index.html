Checkers Rules & Mechanics

1. Movement & Promotion

Regular Pieces: Move 1 diagonal step forward to an unoccupied dark square. Upon reaching the enemy's last row (row 8 for Red, row 1 for Black), the piece is crowned a King.

2. Capturing (Jumping)

Regular Pieces: Can capture forward AND backward! If an adjacent square holds an enemy piece and the square immediately beyond is vacant, the piece jumps over and captures it.

Multi-Jumps: If another jump is immediately available from the landing square, the capturing piece must continue jumping until no jumps remain.

3. Flying Kings (מלכים מעופפים)

Kings can move any number of empty squares diagonally in all 4 directions (like a chess Bishop).

Kings can also jump over an enemy piece from any distance along the diagonal, and land on any vacant square beyond that piece.

4. Mandatory Jumps & Huffing (שריפת חייל)

If any capture is available, the player is expected to jump. If a player attempts a regular step while ignoring an available capture, the opponent has two options:

  • Burn Piece (Huff): The offending piece is immediately removed (burned) from the board as penalty for missing the jump, and turn passes.
  • Demand Mandatory Jump: The move is rolled back and the player is compelled to perform the capture.
